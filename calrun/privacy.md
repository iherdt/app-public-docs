# Privacy Policy for CalRun

**Last updated:** March 9, 2026

## Overview

CalRun is a Caltrain schedule app. Your privacy is important to us.

## Data Collection

CalRun does **not** collect, store, or transmit personal data to a server. Preferences (selected stations and filters) are stored locally on your device.

## No Accounts

CalRun does not require an account, login, or registration.

## No Third-Party Analytics

CalRun does not use third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

## Network Requests

CalRun may make network requests to retrieve transit schedule information (for example, real-time departures). Requests are used only to fetch schedule data and are not used for advertising or tracking.

## In-App Purchases

CalRun may offer optional auto-renewable subscriptions processed through Apple's App Store. Payment information is handled by Apple and is not accessible to us. See [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) for details.

## Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted to this page.
